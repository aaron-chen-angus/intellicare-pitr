# Interactive report update

## Where to confirm the sixth detail

Open **Describe your drawing** in the six-lens section, or click **Confirm detail** on the Figure/composition card. A dedicated Figure/composition field lets you choose the figure's size and its position on a 3×3 page. The marker and summary show the position you selected. All six areas count toward six; posture and the presence of other people no longer substitute for a composition confirmation.

## Label a whole group, not every mark

The process section's main label editor uses a drawing preview. Drag a rectangle around the person, umbrella, rain or other elements, optionally restrict the time interval, and apply one label to the highlighted selection. Region and time filters combine: a mark must match both when both filters are active. You can instead label an entire time interval without drawing a region. The selected count and measured timestamps make it clear what will change, and the last label batch can be undone.

Selection labels whole retained strokes that cross the rectangle, not cut-out pieces of them. A fill is selected by its recorded seedpoint; the app does not pretend the seedpoint measures the whole filled area. Older untimestamped fills cannot participate in a timestamp filter. These edits change labels only, never brush pixels or recorded times. Individual editing remains available under Advanced detail.

## Three activities with an actual takeaway

Choose feelings and their intensity to make a personal word cloud. Match those feelings to colours actually used in your drawing to create your own colour associations. Choose available supports, a trigger and an observable small action to create a practical when/then plan. The interface produces a combined visual summary, progress/completion, and a downloadable keepsake and structured JSON result instead of three blank text boxes. Everything is revised through controls; there is no required free-text answer or paid service.

These activities are custom, voluntary reflection tools informed by practical WHO/NHS guidance and colour-association research. They are not a diagnosis or validated treatment. Their outcomes come from the user's choices, not inferred personality. Activity sources are explained separately from the original nine PITR references, which remain unchanged.

## Deployment

This update must be uploaded to your existing GitHub `docs` folder before your public site changes. The compiled overlay preserves your existing `docs/restyle.config.json` and `docs/restyle/` image assets. Keep the same Pages settings and do not delete `docs` first. The new features require no model call, account or payment; existing artistic-restyling safeguards are unchanged.
